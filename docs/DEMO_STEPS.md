# Demo walkthrough (3-minute video)

Use the private replay link of the real session (kept out of git, printed by `scripts/stage_vercel_preview.sh`), or the fictional demo at https://adfarming-studio-preview.vercel.app/ . In replay the top banner says it is a replay and that nothing calls any AI; say so once.
Sign in with any password. Press **New run**, then **Start**. Screen at 1280 wide, browser zoom 100%.

The replay follows one real session, so whatever you click, it shows what really happened next. Real timings are shown as "That took 3m 1s in the real run". Pauses here are 1 to 3 seconds.
